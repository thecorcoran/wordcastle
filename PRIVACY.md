# Privacy Policy

**Word Castle** (and all apps built on `@word-castle/core`) is designed for children and families with strict privacy:

- **All data stays on your device.** Learning progress, settings, custom words, and parent voice recordings are stored locally in your browser (`localStorage` and `IndexedDB`).
- **No accounts.** There is no sign-up, login, or cloud profile.
- **No analytics or tracking.** We do not collect telemetry, crash reports, usage metrics, or cookies.
- **No network calls.** Once the app is loaded (and cached as a Progressive Web App), it runs completely offline. No audio recordings or text ever leave your device.
- **Backups are yours.** When you export a backup or voice pack, a `.zip` file is generated locally on your device for you to save or share as you choose.
